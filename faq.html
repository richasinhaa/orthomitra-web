Frequently Asked Questions

What is OrthoMitra?

OrthoMitra is a free AI-powered orthopedic guide available on WhatsApp. If you're dealing with any bone, joint, or muscle issue and don't know where to start, just message us. We help you understand your problem, suggest safe exercises, and guide you on when to see a doctor.

Is OrthoMitra free?

Yes, completely free. There are no charges, no subscriptions, and no hidden fees. Just open WhatsApp and start chatting.

Do I need to download an app?

No. OrthoMitra works entirely through WhatsApp. If you already have WhatsApp on your phone, you're ready to go. Just tap the "Chat on WhatsApp" button on our website.

What languages does OrthoMitra support?

OrthoMitra supports 10+ languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Kannada, Gujarati, Malayalam, Punjabi, and English. Just start typing or send a voice note in your preferred language.

Can I send voice messages?

Yes! If typing is difficult or painful, just send a voice note on WhatsApp. OrthoMitra will listen and respond in text or voice — in your language.

Is OrthoMitra a replacement for a doctor?

No. OrthoMitra is an informational guide, not a medical professional. We help you understand your symptoms, suggest safe exercises, and tell you when it's important to see a doctor. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for diagnosis and treatment.

What kind of problems can I ask about?

Anything orthopedic — knee pain, back pain, shoulder stiffness, neck pain, joint pain, arthritis, fractures, sports injuries, post-surgery recovery, and more. If you're not sure what's wrong, that's exactly when you should message us.

Is my data safe?

Your conversations happen on WhatsApp, which uses end-to-end encryption. We do not share your personal health information with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use OrthoMitra at any time?

Yes. OrthoMitra is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Pain doesn't wait for office hours, and neither do we.

How do I get started?

Just tap any "Chat on WhatsApp" button on this website, or message us directly on WhatsApp. Say hi, describe what's bothering you, and we'll take it from there.
